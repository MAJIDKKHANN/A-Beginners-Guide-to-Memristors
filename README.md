# A Beginner's Guide to Memristors

A free, picture-first beginner guide to memristors and ReRAM.

When I started studying memristors, I could not find anything that explained them simply, from the very beginning. Almost everything was a research paper written for people who already knew the topic. So I wrote the guide I wish I had on day one: nine short chapters, each with drawings, an everyday comparison, and small experiments you can play with in your browser.

**Read it here:** https://majidkkhann.github.io/A-Beginners-Guide-to-Memristors/

## Chapters

1. **The Resistor That Remembers** · what a memristor is, in one simple idea
2. **What's Inside** · the metal / oxide / metal sandwich
3. **Building a Tiny Bridge** · forming, SET and RESET
4. **The Bow-Tie Curve** · reading the pinched hysteresis loop
5. **Storing 0s and 1s** · memristors as memory (ReRAM)
6. **Grids of Memristors** · crossbar arrays and sneak paths
7. **Memristors That Do Math** · compute-in-memory for AI
8. **No Two Are the Same** · variability and chip fingerprints (PUFs)
9. **Build One in LTspice** · write and simulate your own memristor model

Plus a word list with every new term in plain language.

## Who it is for

Students in electrical engineering, physics or computer science, and anyone who watched a video about memristors and wants to really understand them. No advanced maths needed.

## About

Written by Majid Khan, electrical engineer (UET Peshawar). My undergraduate thesis was on ReRAM-based physical unclonable functions, which is where Chapter 8 comes from.

Found a mistake or have an idea for a new chapter? Open an issue.
